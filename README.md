# Mirror

**Your mirror self, acting in parallel.**

Mirror is an AI clone of you. It learns your context from your email, Slack and other communication channels, so it knows your people, projects and priorities and how you usually respond.

## What it does

- **Holds conversations for you.** It talks to people on your behalf, drawing on your memory and context.
- **Assigns tasks to agents.** When a conversation needs follow-up work, Mirror hands the tasks to agents so they get done without you.
- **Works through conversation.** You talk to Mirror naturally, by voice or text, with no dashboards or workflows.

## When to use it

Any time your attention is somewhere else, like driving, doing several things at once, or being otherwise tied up. Mirror keeps things moving so the work comes off your plate.
